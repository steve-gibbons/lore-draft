### Synopsis
LORE will **not** adopt, adapt, or model its licensing terms after the IBM Bob International License Agreement for Non-Warranted Programs (ILAN) or equivalent proprietary enterprise product licenses.

The Bob license is a competent commercial instrument for a paid AI SDLC product. It is a poor fit for LORE’s dual objectives of broad adoption as a governance/provenance *standard* and explicit anti-capture.

### Decision summary (to become normative on acceptance)
1. **Reject** the IBM Bob ILAN (and close analogues) as a model or source of terms for LORE licensing.
2. **Reaffirm** the direction already recorded in `DECISION-LICENSING.accepted.md`:
   - Near-term: Provisional Evaluation license.
   - Long-haul: Apache-2.0 (code/tooling) + CC BY 4.0 (specification/docs), triggered at v1.0.0 and gated on governance lock-in.
3. **Capture** findings F-01 … F-06 as inputs to the remaining legal-polish and anti-capture control work. These are not reasons to reconsider the Bob model.

### Findings (remain candidate implications until author triage)
| ID | Finding | Proposed disposition |
|----|---------|----------------------|
| F-01 | Clean separation of Separately Licensed Code / third-party + AI-origin material | Address in provisional license legal review + CONTRIBUTING / third-party notice |
| F-02 | Narrow reverse-engineering / modification rights vs LORE’s permissive posture | Explicit permitted-evaluation list in provisional text |
| F-03 | Strong verification / audit rights | Cross-link NEW-04; ensure license does not restrict legitimate independent verification |

| F-06 | No machinery for recording *why* terms exist | Process norm already consistent with LORE decision practice |
