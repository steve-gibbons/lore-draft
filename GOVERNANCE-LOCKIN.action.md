## Definition of done - "governance lock-in"
The v1.0.0 relicense gate opens when **all** of the following are true and recorded in the governed
corpus:
1. A governance model is chosen and written down (structure, decision process, amendment rules).
2. Named participants have accepted defined roles - at minimum a succession path beyond the founder.
3. A functioning organization exists at the rigor the participants agreed (from a lightweight
   stewardship charter to a formal entity - the level is the participants' call, not prescribed here).
4. Rights and responsibilities are assigned and accepted: authority to publish canonical LORE,
   trademark / name stewardship, conformance authority, and contribution / amendment rights.

Until all four hold, LORE stays under the Provisional Evaluation license.

## Known requirements & commitments (already established - carry these in)
These are inputs, not open questions; they constrain whatever model is formed:
- **Succession (LH-R4 / KF-05):** authority must survive its founder; a transfer model must exist.
- **Reciprocal accountability (LH-R2):** authority itself must be auditable - sousveillance of the
  watcher, not only the governed.
- **Anti-capture (LH-R3):** resist takeover across stewards and time; no single actor co-opts LORE.
- **Bounded accretion (LH-R5):** consolidation without erasing provenance as the corpus grows.
- **Anti-capture control stack (from the licensing decision), to be *owned* by the governance body:**
  - *Trademark / name policy* - reserve "LORE" and marks; nominative-use carve-out; gate
    "official / certified / LORE-compatible" claims on conformance.
  - *Conformance governance* - published, versioned tests defining compatibility.
  - *Specification versioning* - anyone may fork under CC BY; only an authorized steward publishes a
    version as *canonical* LORE.
- **Contribution / evolution process (KF-14):** a contributor / RFC path wiring outside input into
  the proposal / decision records, plus a model-versioning / deprecation policy.

## The plan (owner action)
Assigned to the Author. Steps run in order; the *rigor* is deliberately left to the participants
(step 3), per the requirement that local governance not be over-prescribed from the top.
1. **Identify governance models.** Survey candidate models (e.g. BDFL-with-succession, steering
   committee, stewardship council, fiscal-host / foundation, standards-body-style working group) and
   their fit against the requirements above. → *Output:* a shortlist with trade-offs.
2. **Identify potential governance participants.** Name candidate stewards / contributors and the
   constituencies they represent. → *Output:* an invite list, with the role each is considered for.
3. **Form the governance organization** at whatever level of rigor the participants decide - floor is
   a written stewardship charter naming a succession path; ceiling is a formal legal entity.
   → *Output:* a constituted body with a recorded charter.
4. **Assign rights and responsibilities** as appropriate and agreed: canonical-publish authority,
   trademark / name stewardship, conformance authority, contribution / amendment rights, and the
   succession / transfer mechanism. → *Output:* a rights-and-responsibilities record accepted by the
   participants.

On completion, record the outcome as an accepted decision and flip the v1.0.0 gate to
"governance locked."
