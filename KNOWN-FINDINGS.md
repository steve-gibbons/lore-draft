## Wave-2 findings in brief
**KF-12 (Records/Legal, C6):** LORE preserves everything (supersede-not-delete) but has no
retention schedule, disposition authority, or legal-hold concept - records-management essentials.
**KF-13 (Enterprise, C2):** No APIs/export formats/mappings to enterprise systems (IAM, GRC,
CMDB), no "minimal viable adoption" path, no scale model beyond flat-files + one validator.
**KF-14 (Standards/OSS, C5):** "Sole authority" is the opposite of sustainable community
governance - no contributor/RFC process wiring the proposal/decision records to outside input,
no model-versioning/deprecation policy, and the public draft has **no LICENSE** (an easy fix).
**KF-15 (Data-Arch, C7):** It's file-state-based, not event-sourced, despite provenance/history
goals - Act-vs-State is unmodeled, there are no projections (current-accepted vs full-history),
and concurrency/merge semantics are undefined (git merge isn't governance-aware).

## Findings in detail (wave-1)

**KF-01 · Declarative, not authenticated.** Author-only status gated by a self-written field
`provenance.author_preseeded` (`TOOLS/lore_validate.py` Check 2); author identity is
unauthenticated git `user.email`. A provenance system that records authority claims without
binding them to something unforgeable can be escalated by any writer or adversarial INTAKE.

**KF-02 · No enforcement at rest.** Checks run only when the validator is invoked; the hook is
not installed (and rejects the mode-644 `INTAKE/raw/README.md` the validator exempts). *Quick
win:* fix the hook's README exemption + run the validator in CI.

**KF-03 · Parser-differential.** Originally a pure hand-rolled parser. Current posture: dual-path load (PyYAML preferred, lore_yaml fallback, fail-closed); output pinned to lore_yaml only. Residual recorded and accepted in `META-CONTEXT/kf-03-parser-differential.accepted.md`.

**KF-04 · TCB undefined.** LORE never names its minimal trusted invariants - what the design
source ("define the TCB for LORE") asked for. Everything else is harder to secure until it exists. (Now addressed by CORE-INVARIANTS.md.)

**KF-05 · Governance-across-time unbuilt.** No succession for "sole authority," no
consolidation/decay, authority not itself auditable. *Quick win:* promote the long-horizon
rationale (currently un-promoted) into the governed corpus.

**KF-06 · Integrity advisory, not forensic.** `chmod a-w` reversible & not git-preserved; hash
check opt-in; no append-only tamper-evident log; break-glass unenforced.

**KF-07 · Agent consumption not realizable.** `integration-contract` is a FUTURE stub; no
validated context-package, no machine-enforced generated-vs-knowledge signal, tool/capability
delegation out of scope.

**KF-08 · Ontology thin/conflated.** Canonical kernel advanced the core distinctions. Residual (remote resolution, single status field load, formal glossary) recorded and accepted in `META-CONTEXT/kf-08-ontology-residual.accepted.md`; object-ref schema tightened.

**KF-09 · Prior art unmapped.** Crosswalk to PROV-O, OAIS, ISO 15489 and event-sourcing accepted in `META-CONTEXT/prior-art-crosswalk.accepted.md`.

**KF-10 · Complexity risk.** Growing surface (status enum, transition matrix, schemas,
indirection) - "abstraction expansion" from the minimalist lens.

**KF-11 · AFFIRM - protect these.** Separation of commonly-conflated concepts
(identity/authority/capability, OBJECT vs OBJECT_REF, evidence vs knowledge, generated vs
canonical); supersede-not-delete; preserved rationale & uncertainty; a dependency-free, small
validator. Five lenses independently affirmed these.
