# LORE Long-Horizon Rationale (proposed / DRAFT)

> **Status: accepted** (agent-drafted under author direction). NOT normative until accepted.
> EXPERIMENTAL / provisional. Promotes LORE's own long-horizon rationale into the governed corpus
> — dogfooding the thesis "preserve the *why*." Addresses **KF-05**.

## Provenance
- **inputs:** the design source (held in the private canonical corpus) and the P6 long-horizon
  self-assessment finding (`KNOWN-FINDINGS.md`).
- **transformation:** promotion of ungoverned design rationale into a governed requirements record.

## Why this document exists
LORE's stated purpose is that future maintainers can recover *why* something exists. The P6
self-assessment found LORE's own long-horizon rationale lived only in an ungoverned design
conversation — un-promoted. This record promotes it, so LORE preserves its own reason-for-being
by its own rules.

## Long-horizon requirements (governed)
- **LH-R1 Multi-generational legibility** — preserve rationale, decisions, and uncertainty so
  maintainers decades out can reconstruct the *why*. — *partially realized*
- **LH-R2 Reciprocal accountability** — authority itself must be auditable (sousveillance of the
  watcher), not only the governed. — *open (KF-05)*
- **LH-R3 Anti-capture** — resist takeover across stewards and time.
- **LH-R4 Continuity / succession** — authority must survive its founder; a transfer model exists.
  — *open (KF-05)*
- **LH-R5 Bounded accretion** — remain legible as the corpus grows; consolidation without erasing
  provenance. — *open (KF-05)*
- **LH-R6 Vendor / technology neutrality** — durable, migratable, exit-able; no single-vendor lock.
  — *partially realized (plain-text + git)*

## Status
LH-R1 and LH-R6 are partially realized by current design. LH-R2, LH-R4, LH-R5 are **open** —
theorized but unbuilt. This record makes them first-class governed requirements rather than lost
conversation, and is the seed of a future long-horizon governance model.
