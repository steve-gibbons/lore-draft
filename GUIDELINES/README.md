# GUIDELINES

**Strong guidelines** for LORE - advisory-strong principles that shape design and review but are
**not** hard, validator-enforced invariants (those live in `CORE-INVARIANTS.md`). A guideline that
later warrants enforcement graduates to an invariant.

## Convention
- `candidates/` - proposed guidelines; status `candidate` / `proposed`. The author accepts to make
  a guideline `accepted` (promote out of `candidates/`). **Author-only** promotion.
- Each guideline records: the **truism**, **known failure patterns**, **attribution** (borrowed vs.
  novel to LORE), how LORE **heeds it internally**, and its disposition / provenance.
